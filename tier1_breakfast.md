# Make Me Breakfast

## Breakfast: Fried Eggs and Toasted Bread

### Ingredients
- 2 eggs
- 4 slices of bread
- 4 teaspoons of cooking oil
- 1/4 teaspoon of salt

### Equipment
- Frying pan
- Spatula
- Plate
- Stove
- Egg bowl

### Instructions

1. Check that all required ingredients and equipment are available.
2. Wash your hands before handling food.
3. Crack the two eggs into the egg bowl.
4. Add 1/4 teaspoon of salt and mix the eggs.
5. Place the frying pan on the stove.
6. Add 4 teaspoons of cooking oil to the pan.
7. Turn on the stove using moderate heat.
8. Allow the oil to heat.
9. Carefully pour the beaten eggs into the frying pan.
10. Cook the eggs, turning them with the spatula, until they are properly cooked.
11. Toast the four slices of bread.
12. Place the cooked eggs and toasted bread on a clean plate.
13. Turn off the stove.
14. Place the prepared breakfast on the dining table.

### Completion Conditions

The task is complete when:
- The eggs are properly cooked.
- The bread is toasted.
- Both are served on a clean plate.
- The stove is turned off.
