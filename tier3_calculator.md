# Tier 3: Input → Processing → Output

## 1. Addition

- Input: 20 and 5
- Processing: 20 + 5
- Output: 25

## 2. Subtraction

- Input: 20 and 5
- Processing: 20 - 5
- Output: 15

## 3. Multiplication

- Input: 20 and 5
- Processing: 20 × 5
- Output: 100

## 4. Division

- Input: 20 and 5
- Processing: 20 ÷ 5
- Output: 4

## 5. Division by Zero

If the second number is zero, the calculator should display an error message instead of attempting division.

## Conclusion

A calculator receives numbers as input, performs mathematical operations on them, and displays the result as output.
